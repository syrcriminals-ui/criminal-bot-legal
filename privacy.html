CRIMNAL Bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Information We Collect

CRIMNAL Bot may process basic Discord information required for its features, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, usernames, and interaction data.

Information is used only to provide and operate the bot's features, maintain functionality, and prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal information. Data is not shared with third parties except when required to operate the service or comply with applicable law.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the bot's functionality.

5. Data Removal

Users may request deletion of stored information by contacting the CRIMNAL Bot team through the official Discord server.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the bot.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the CRIMNAL Bot team through the official Discord server.
